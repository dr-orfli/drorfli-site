Overview

Rixos Radamis Sharm El Sheikh

Ultra All-Inclusive Luxury on the Red Sea

Nestled along the pristine shores of Nabq Bay, Rixos Radamis Sharm El Sheikh delivers an Ultra All-Inclusive experience that redefines effortless luxury. Every meal, every drink, every activity, and every evening show is woven into a single, uninterrupted rhythm of indulgence.

With 336 elegantly appointed rooms and suites, a 600-metre private beach, seven swimming pools, a dedicated aqua park, and the tranquil Anjana Spa, the resort is engineered to satisfy the most discerning traveller — families, couples, and solo explorers alike.

From the moment you arrive until the final farewell, every need is anticipated and every detail is perfected. This is not simply a hotel. It is a destination complete unto itself.

Beach

600 Metres of Private Red Sea Beach

The resort's private beach stretches 600 metres along the Red Sea — soft white sand, calm turquoise waters, and a carefully curated beach experience from sunrise to sunset. Complimentary sunbeds and parasols are assigned each morning on a first-come basis. Beach towels are exchanged daily at the dedicated towel station.

  • Complimentary sunbeds & parasols
  • Beach towel exchange service
  • Watersports centre (jet ski, banana boat, kayak — extra charge)
  • Snorkelling equipment available at the beach hut (included)
  • Beach bar service
  • Lifeguard supervision during beach hours
  • Non-motorised water sports area clearly demarcated

Due to Red Sea regulations, personal jet skis and external watersports operators are not permitted on the private beach.

🕐 Beach open 08:00–18:30 | Beach bar until 17:00

Weather

October to April — warm, dry, and uncrowded relative to summer. November and April offer the best balance of weather, crowd levels, and pricing.

Month ☀️ High °C 🌡 Low °C 🌊 Sea °C Note
January 22°14°22° Warm days, cool evenings. Perfect for sightseeing.
February 23°15°21° Similar to January — mild and sunny.
March 26°17°22° Spring warmth arrives. Sea begins to warm.
April 30°20°24° Excellent weather. Best shoulder-season value.
May 34°23°26° Hot and sunny. Ideal for beach & water activities.
June 37°26°28° Peak heat. Stay hydrated. Evening activities recommended.
July 38°27°29° Hottest month. Excellent for diving — warm clear water.
August 38°27°30° Similar to July. Sea at its warmest.
September 35°25°29° Slightly cooler. Still excellent beach weather.
October 31°22°27° Post-peak sweet spot. Great diving visibility.
November 27°19°25° Comfortable temperatures. Ideal for all ages.
December 23°15°23° Mild and quiet. Good value season.

☀️ July and August are very hot (38°C+) but the sea is at its most inviting. Shade and air-conditioning breaks are essential. The resort's pools and aqua park become extremely popular.
