Do you need to reserve the water park?

The honest answer
Access to Aquamania Jungle Park is included in your Ultra All-Inclusive stay — no separate ticket, no entry fee. Official sources state the park is bundled into the single all-inclusive rate. General access is free-flow; certain slides simply have the usual height/age safety rules, confirmed on the spot by the aquapark team. A food court and kiosks let you stay all day.

Operating hours: daily hours are seasonal; I recommend confirming the day’s schedule with Guest Relations on arrival (contact details on the Links page). Built by ProSlide, a global leader in water-ride design.
